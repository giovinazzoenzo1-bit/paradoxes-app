# Calendrier : pièces (étape 2, case par case)

Dans la MÊME conversation Gemini que la maquette du Calendrier. Déposer ici, DANS L'ORDRE, les images 52 à 55.

Lien : https://github.com/giovinazzoenzo1-bit/paradoxes-app/upload/main/design/a-integrer/10-defis-progres/pieces-calendrier

Réutilisés : panneau des fenêtres, bannière, croix, rail + remplissage de barre, grand bouton vert, pattes, cristal, médaillon coché. États « pris » et « du jour » faits par le code. Grille réelle : 3 petites cases, puis 2 lignes de 2 cases larges (identique à la maquette).

**52. Petite case (vide)**

```
From the calendar screen you just made, draw ONLY one SMALL day box of the top row (like 'Jour 3'), plain wood with its mossy corners, completely EMPTY: no icon, no text, no golden border, no check mark. About 1.3 units wide for 1 unit tall, in a 4:3 image. Centered and fully visible with empty space around it, same style, on a perfectly flat, uniform, pure magenta background (#FF00FF), no gradient, no texture. No drop shadow, no glow and no light around the object. Never use pink, purple or magenta on the object itself.
```

**53. Grande case (vide)**

```
From the calendar screen you just made, draw ONLY one WIDE day box of the lower rows (like 'Jour 4'), plain wood with its mossy corners, completely EMPTY: no icon, no text. About 2.2 units wide for 1 unit tall, in a 16:9 image. Centered and fully visible with empty space around it, same style, on a perfectly flat, uniform, pure magenta background (#FF00FF), no gradient, no texture. No drop shadow, no glow and no light around the object. Never use pink, purple or magenta on the object itself.
```
