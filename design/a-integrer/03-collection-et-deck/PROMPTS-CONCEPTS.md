# Collection : 3 concepts « pas un menu classique » (02/10)

Joindre 2 images : IMAGE 1 = capture du MENU PRINCIPAL ; IMAGE 2 = capture de TA Collection actuelle (avec tes créatures). Puis UN prompt par concept, chacun dans une NOUVELLE conversation.

Réutilisable : les 8 cadres de cartes par élément (actuel/) — le concept « Album de cartes » s'appuie dessus.

Lien : https://github.com/giovinazzoenzo1-bit/paradoxes-app/upload/main/design/a-integrer/03-collection-et-deck/concepts

**1. L'Album de cartes (recommandé)**

```
I'm sending you 2 images. IMAGE 1 is my mobile game's main menu: it is the STYLE REFERENCE (hand-painted cozy magical forest, weathered wood, moss, vines, blue-grey pebbles, golden accents, glowing cyan mushrooms). IMAGE 2 is the current, old COLLECTION screen (the creatures the player collects). Redesign this COLLECTION screen in the exact style of IMAGE 1, NOT a classic grid menu: a big open card album lying on a mossy tree stump. Each page holds 6 trading-card sleeves (3 columns, 2 rows). Each creature is a collectible card with an ornate frame colored by its element (fire: red-orange; water: blue; earth: green and brown roots; air: silver-white; lightning: dark blue with purple lightning; light: gold and ivory; darkness: black and dark purple; magic: violet with runes), the creature illustration, its name and level, and small gems for its rarity (1 to 6 gems). Undiscovered creatures are face-down cards showing a dark silhouette and '???'. Along the right edge of the album, 8 small colored tabs, one per element, to jump between pages. Above the album, a strip of 3 card slots labeled 'DECK' (the creatures taken on adventure). Below the album, a big glowing golden egg button labeled 'Invoquer' with its price in coins. Keep the same top bar as IMAGE 1 (RETOUR wooden sign top-left, coins and diamonds counters top-right) and the same bottom navigation bar (SHOP / COLLECTION / EXPLORATION). Vertical phone screen 9:16. Everything readable, lots of breathing space, nothing overlapping. Texts in French.
```
