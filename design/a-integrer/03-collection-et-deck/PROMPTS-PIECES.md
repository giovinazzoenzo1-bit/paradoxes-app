# Série 3 (02/10) — l'abri en PAYSAGE

L'image 74 est revenue presque carrée : le prompt imposait un cadre PORTRAIT 9:16, que Gemini remplit. Pour un objet large, demander un cadre PAYSAGE 16:9.

**76. L'abri du deck, LARGE et BAS (cadre paysage)**

```
From the COLLECTION screen you just made (the card album), draw ONLY the DECK shelter exactly as it appears in the screen, as a WIDE and LOW structure: it must be about 2.6 times wider than tall, like a long market stall seen from the front: a long mossy wooden roof with vines across its whole width, a short wooden post at each end, FOUR lanterns hanging outside the posts (two on each side, lit inside their glass, no light halo), and a small BLANK wooden sign in the middle of the roof (no text). Below the roof, a long, low, dark wooden back panel with THREE EMPTY card places side by side, well spaced (no cards, no '+'). No base, no floor, no book under it. Same style. Horizontal LANDSCAPE image, 16:9; the object fills most of the width and is fully visible, on a perfectly flat, uniform, pure magenta background (#FF00FF), no gradient, no texture. No drop shadow, no glow, no light rays and no halo around the object. Never use pink or magenta on the object itself.
```


# Série 4 (02/10) — les 8 marque-pages de la maquette

Demande de l'auteur : marque-pages identiques à la maquette, en UN prompt. Sans texte : le code écrit FEU, EAU…, LUMIÈRE, TÉNÈBRES (orthographe et accents garantis, lisibles à petite taille), dans le style de la maquette.

**77. Les 8 marque-pages d'éléments**

```
From the COLLECTION screen you just made (the card album), draw ONLY the 8 element tabs that stick out of the right edge of the album, EXACTLY in the same style as in the screen (same shape, same painted look, same border, same shine), but WITHOUT any text and without the album: 8 tabs of the same shape and size, each in its element color, from top to bottom: 1 fire = red-orange, 2 water = blue, 3 earth = green, 4 air = light silver-white, 5 lightning = dark indigo blue, 6 light = golden yellow, 7 darkness = dark grey, almost black, 8 magic = violet-purple. Arrange them in ONE vertical column, in this order, all aligned, with clear empty space between them so that they do NOT touch each other. Same style. Vertical PORTRAIT image, 9:16; the column is centered and fully visible, on a perfectly flat, uniform, pure magenta background (#FF00FF), no gradient, no texture. No drop shadow, no glow, no light rays and no halo. Never use pink or magenta on the tabs themselves.
```
