# Tutoriel : le rond et le carré qui montrent « clique ici »

Déposer ici, DANS L'ORDRE, les images des prompts 110 à 112.

Lien : https://github.com/giovinazzoenzo1-bit/paradoxes-app/upload/main/design/a-integrer/19-tuto

**Mode d'emploi** : NOUVELLE conversation Gemini. Au prompt 110, joindre une capture de l'écran principal du jeu (référence
de style). Puis 111 et 112 dans la MÊME conversation. Le rond, l'étoile et le carré sont 3 images SÉPARÉES : le rond tourne,
les étoiles ont leur propre effet (elles ne tournent pas avec lui), la lueur est ajoutée par le jeu.

**110. Le rond (il tournera)**

```
The image I'm sending is a screenshot of my mobile game: it is the STYLE REFERENCE. Draw ONLY one ring that will be used to show the player where to tap: a thin bright golden ring made of two curved arcs with two small gaps between them, like a magic circle drawn with a quick brush stroke, polished and shiny with small white highlights on the gold, a thin darker golden outline, the inside of the ring completely empty. Square 1:1 image. Centered and fully visible with empty space around it, same style, on a perfectly flat, uniform, pure magenta background (#FF00FF), no gradient, no texture. The inside of the ring is the same flat magenta background. No stars, no sparkles, no text, no letters, no numbers. No drop shadow, no glow and no light around the object. Never use pink, purple or magenta on the object itself.
```

**111. La petite étoile (séparée du rond, elle scintillera)**

```
In the same style as the previous image, draw ONLY one small four-pointed sparkle star, bright golden-yellow with a white shiny center, thin darker golden outline, slightly curved points, alone. Square 1:1 image. Centered and fully visible with empty space around it, same style, on a perfectly flat, uniform, pure magenta background (#FF00FF), no gradient, no texture. No text, no letters, no numbers. No drop shadow, no glow and no light around the object. Never use pink, purple or magenta on the object itself.
```

**112. Le carré (pour entourer un défi, un bouton, une zone)**

```
In the same style as the previous images, draw ONLY one square frame that will be used to highlight a part of the screen: a thin bright golden band of EVEN thickness all around, slightly rounded corners, polished and shiny with small white highlights, thin darker golden outline, completely empty inside, with a tiny decorative golden notch at the middle of each side. The frame must stay simple so it can be stretched into a long rectangle. Square 1:1 image. Centered and fully visible with empty space around it, same style, on a perfectly flat, uniform, pure magenta background (#FF00FF), no gradient, no texture. The inside of the frame is the same flat magenta background. No stars, no sparkles, no text, no letters, no numbers. No drop shadow, no glow and no light around the object. Never use pink, purple or magenta on the object itself.
```
