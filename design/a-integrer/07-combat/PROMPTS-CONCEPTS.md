# Combat : 3 concepts SOBRES et ÉPURÉS (03/10)

Écran en PAYSAGE. Éléments relevés dans le code (CombatScreen.js) : ✕ (quitter) ; badges selon le cas (🧪 Élixir : ennemis −10 % ; 🐯 GARDIEN) ;
équipe (3) et adversaires (1 à 3) avec nom, vie et mana ; dégâts flottants ; message de tour (Au tour de X ! / X provoque ! / Bouclier, Soin, Boost) ;
attaque normale (icône, nom, dégâts) ; SPÉCIALE (description, coût en mana, dégâts, grisée sans mana) ; 📺 Recharge ;
moments du Gardien (LE GARDIEN SE RELÈVE, défi de taps avec chrono) gérés par le code. Fin de combat (CombatResultScreen) : écran à part (08).

Joindre 3 images : IMAGE 1 = capture du MENU PRINCIPAL ; IMAGE 2 = capture d'un COMBAT actuel (en paysage) ; IMAGE 3 = capture du nouvel APERÇU « Le médaillon ».
Une NOUVELLE conversation par concept. Dépôt (ordre 1, 2, 3) : https://github.com/giovinazzoenzo1-bit/paradoxes-app/upload/main/design/a-integrer/07-combat/concepts

**1. Le duel épuré (recommandé)**

```
I'm sending you 3 images. IMAGE 1 is my mobile game's main menu: the STYLE REFERENCE (hand-painted cozy magical forest, weathered wood, moss, golden accents). IMAGE 3 is the LEVEL PREVIEW screen that comes just before the fight (wooden planks, golden medallions, golden button): this new screen must belong to the SAME family. IMAGE 2 is the current, old FIGHT screen, shown in LANDSCAPE in the game. Redesign this FIGHT screen as a SOBER, CLEAN, MINIMAL screen, very readable during the action. Keep EXACTLY these elements and nothing more: top-left, a small round wooden button with a '✕' (to quit the fight); the player's team of 3 creatures on the LEFT and the enemy creatures on the RIGHT (1 to 3 enemies: leave room for 3), all standing firmly ON THE GROUND; under each creature, its name and two thin gauges: a HEALTH bar with its numbers (for example '42 / 60') and a small blue MANA bar; above each enemy, a small round ELEMENT badge (an element icon in its color); a short turn message in the top center, for example 'Au tour de Pyrosile !'; one floating damage number '-4' above the hit enemy; and the ATTACKS of the active creature: one NORMAL attack ('Morsure Chaude', '4 dégâts') and one SPECIAL attack ('Zone', '40 % à tous', '3 mana'), the special one clearly greyed out when there is not enough mana, plus a small '📺 Recharge' button. Layout: a calm battle clearing in the style of IMAGE 1, with a wide flat ground; the two teams face each other across it, the active creature of each side slightly in front. The gauges sit on small wooden plaques under the creatures. The attacks are two big wooden cards at the bottom right, side by side, with the '📺 Recharge' button just above them. No other button, no other text, no extra decoration. Horizontal LANDSCAPE phone screen, 16:9. Everything readable, nothing overlapping. Texts in French.
```
